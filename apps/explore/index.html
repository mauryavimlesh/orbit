Scientific foundations & limitations

ORBIT's numerical model solves Newtonian point-mass two-body relative motion using velocity Verlet integration. Distances are in astronomical units and time in years; the normalizing gravitational parameter is 4π² AU³/year². The star is drawn at the origin of the relative separation coordinates; this is not a barycentric trajectory. The demo intentionally excludes perturbations, relativity, oblateness and atmospheric effects; collision radius is illustrative.

References: NASA Solar System Science · OpenStax University Physics Vol. 1 (Gravitation). Sources are general references; this demo has not completed independent subject-matter review.
